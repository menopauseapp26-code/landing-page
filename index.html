Knowledge is power

A quiet place to write down how your body's doing.

morrow is a private journal for symptoms, sleep, cycles, and the things you try along the way — the small, hard-to-remember details that add up to a clearer picture over time.

What you can journal

◐

Symptoms

Note what you're feeling, when it started, and how it changes — in your own words, on your own schedule.

☾

Sleep

Track how you slept and how you felt after, without wearing a device or syncing an account.

◌

Periods

Log your cycle alongside everything else, so patterns across symptoms, sleep, and timing become easier to see.

+

Treatments

Keep a running list of what you've tried — supplements, medications, habits — and note what's worked and what hasn't.

Your entries stay on your phone

morrow doesn't have a server. There's no account to create and nothing to sync. What you write is stored locally on your device — not uploaded, not backed up to the cloud, not shared with anyone, including us.

That means no one can see your journal but you. If you delete the app, your entries go with it, so it's worth knowing that trade-off going in.
